## Prompt 1 — sobre_abriendo_1.png (Solapa 25% abierta)

Professional product photograph of a single dark olive green matte paper envelope, diamond/euro flap style. The envelope is viewed from directly above (top-down flat lay) and is rotated approximately 15-20 degrees clockwise (tilted to the right).

The triangular flap is SLIGHTLY LIFTED — approximately 25% open. The flap has just begun to separate from the body. The flap tip rises about 1-2cm above the envelope surface, creating a subtle shadow underneath. The flap's hinge line is along the top edge of the envelope body.

CRITICAL DETAILS:
- The interior of the flap is visible at its lifted edge — same dark olive green color but slightly lighter/warmer due to less shadow
- A thin shadow is cast by the lifted flap onto the envelope body below
- The rest of the envelope body (front pocket, side flaps, bottom flap) remains completely flat and unchanged
- NO wax seal, NO card visible, NO text
- The paper texture is matte, slightly fibrous, dark olive/forest green (#3B4332)
- Transparent/empty background (PNG cutout, no surface underneath)
- The envelope is the EXACT same size, angle, and position as the reference image
- Photorealistic quality, studio lighting from upper-left
- Canvas size: 1792 x 2400 pixels
- The envelope should be centered in the canvas, occupying roughly the middle 60% of the vertical space
